About This Market

Organic Corner in PECHS caters to health-conscious families looking for clean, chemical-free food. Operating on weekends, local farmers bring freshly harvested spinach, broccoli, hydroponic lettuce, and fragrant herbs, alongside stone-ground flour and artisanal cheeses.
